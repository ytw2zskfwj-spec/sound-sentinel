    Good to know

    Sound Sentinel is a helper, not a replacement for certified smoke or carbon monoxide alarms. It only listens while the app is open and the screen is on. Set Auto-Lock to Never in iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness, and use Bedside mode overnight.
